## 5. Takeaways for an upgrade (recommendations only)

1. **Minimum stop.** Trades risking 1 pt or less lost 10.25R; 2-4 pt stops made +9.98R and +$1,621.
   Skip signals with a stop under ~2 pts (or widen the stop to a 2 pt floor). That moves CRT Pro
   toward the course's 3.5-4 pt rule. Also cap the maximum at ~6-8 pts: the 2 trades over 8 pts
   cost $2,142.
2. **A fixed 4 pt stop / 4 pt target was the only rule that made money**: +34.8 pts, +$2,105 on
   2 ES after costs, 53% win, losing streak 5 vs 17. It's thin, about $15 a trade; on MES the costs
   eat it (-$84). Worth adding as a test mode, not as the default yet.
3. **Session filter.** London carried the results (+10.4R, +$1,165); Asia (-11.2R) and NY PM
   (-6.4R) lost under every rule except the 4/4 bracket. Tim's 10-12 window was positive
   (+5.3R, n=17, tentative). Skip 9:30-10:00 opening signals (3 trades, -$2,450 gross).
4. **Longs beat shorts** over these 3 days (longs +$5,490 on the 4/4 bracket, shorts -$3,385).
   With n of about 70 each that's a trend, not a rule; watch it, it may just be the market's path.
5. **BE after TP1 is not the fix it looked like on 10/1.** On real bars it is about flat on R and
   worse in $, because it cuts 5 TP3 winners. Keep the TP3 runner logic unless a minimum stop
   changes the picture.

Confidence: moderate on the stop-size finding (it holds across buckets and rules), low on
sessions and direction (3 trading days, 20-44 trades per cell). About 300 trades (2 more weeks at
the current ~45/day) would firm up the session calls. The VMap test needs 30+ trades before any
read.

### Verdict

The 4/4 bracket beats the current exits (+$2,105 vs -$4,117 on 2 ES) and it's not one lucky
setting. But the edge is thin (PF about 1.1), it came from a single day (10/1), it lost on 10/2,
and it doesn't survive MES costs. The session filter improves the in-sample numbers slightly (PF
1.20) but fails the one split test available. Recommendation: don't switch live. Log 4/4 as a
shadow result on every CRT Pro trade for 2 more weeks (about 400-500 trades), then re-check by
day and session, using the first week to pick the sessions and the second to test them.
Confidence: low.
