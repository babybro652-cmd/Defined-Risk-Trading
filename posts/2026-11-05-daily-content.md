## 9:00 AM Facebook (page 993318963868124)
```
You don't need to win most of your trades.

Plenty of traders chase a high win rate and ignore how big their losses get. Run the numbers instead.

Take 10 trades. 4 are winners that make 2R each. 6 are losers that cost 1R each. That's +8R against -6R, so you finish +2R before fees, with a 40% win rate.

R is the amount you risk on a trade. With a 4-point stop on ES, 1R is 4 points.

The catch: this only works if every loss stays the size you planned. Move the stop, let a 1R loss grow into 3R, and the math falls apart.

Protect the loss first. The winners handle the rest.

Follow Defined Risk Trading if you want the math behind your stops.
```
