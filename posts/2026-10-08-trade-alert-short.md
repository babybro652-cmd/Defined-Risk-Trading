TITLE
Thursday 10/8 Trade Recap: I Hit Buy Instead of Sell

POST
This morning's bias was short, and I traded it twice. The first trade ended on a mis-click. The second one is why the plan matters.

Trade 1: I shorted 2 ES at 7,842.75. My plan was to add 2 more at 7,838.00 as price moved my way. When it got there, I hit BUY instead of SELL. That closed both contracts at 7,838.00. +4.75 points each, +$475, but not the trade I planned.

Trade 2: I didn't chase it and I didn't revenge trade. I waited for price to give me another short, re-entered 2 at 7,838.25, added 2 more at 7,835.25 as it moved my way, and closed all 4 at 7,834.75. +3.50 on the first 2, +0.50 on the add, +$400.

Total: 17.5 contract-points, +$875 before commissions.

THE LESSON: ORDER-ENTRY DISCIPLINE
• Check BUY vs SELL before you tap. Read the button, every time, even when you're moving fast.
• Use bracket or ATM orders so your stop and target go in with the entry. Adds should be planned orders, not a rushed manual tap.
• A mistake isn't a reason to force the next trade. Reset, wait for the CRT setup to line up with the bias again, then get back in.
• Count every fill. My result came from the rules, not from the mis-click.

DISCUSSION
What's your process for placing orders fast without hitting the wrong button? Do you use brackets/ATM, or enter by hand?
