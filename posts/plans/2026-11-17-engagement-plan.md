## 2. Diagnosis in one paragraph
DRT's engagement comes from one thing: Tim's real chart with a plain trade hook and a twist. That format gets 4x the views
and 2x the engagement rate of text cards on TikTok, all the IG/FB/YouTube winners, nearly all comments and every YouTube
subscriber. Text cards fill 60-80% of the feed, get few comments, never ask the viewer anything, open on a static frame, and
since 11/11 TikTok has stopped spreading the account (median 87, 0.5% engagement, even on trade reels). The fix is more
of Tim's chart in short, low-effort clips, fewer templated cards, a question at the end of each caption, and a reply habit.

### 3. Give people a reason to comment, and answer them
Why: 0 captions ask the viewer anything; comments only come from trade twists; TikTok replies are unchecked since 11/11.
How:
- **End every caption with one specific, easy question** (not "comment below", not "tag a friend", which stay banned). Examples:
  - "Would you have taken the second short, or stopped after the first?"
  - "Where's your stop on this one: 2 points, 4, or 8?"
  - "Do you trade before 10 AM or wait?"
  - "What's your max loss for a day?"
  Questions with 2-3 choices get answered most. Drop "Save this" / "Follow for" (no measurable lift).
- **Reply routine:**
  - TikTok (Blotato can't read it): Tim, 5 min at 10 AM lunch or after bedtime, 3-4 days a week. Reply to every question
    within 24 h; a question worth a video gets "answering this in a video". Copy any comment he wants help with into chat;
    Jarvis drafts the reply.
  - IG/FB: Jarvis checks `list_comments` daily in the 5 AM run. Option A: Jarvis drafts, Tim approves. Option B (needs his
    OK): Jarvis replies on autopilot to non-trade comments (thanks, how-to questions answered with the rules), and holds
    anything about a trade, money, or a personal question for Tim.
- **Pin** the best comment (or a "Skool: Defined Risk Trading" comment) on each TikTok trade reel. In-app only, 30 s.
- **Reply videos weekly** (change 2): the only format built from what the audience asked.
