## New angles to test (8)

Ranked by expected impact. Each runs at least twice in the 2 weeks so there's something to compare.

1. **"Why I passed"** (needs footage). A 30-60 s screen recording of a setup Tim watched and skipped, with his reason ("it swept the low before 10 AM, so I wasn't in it" / "the stop would've been 9 points, my max is 4"). No P&L, so it posts on autopilot. It shows the discipline the brand sells, and it's content on days with no trade.
2. **Reply with video to a real comment** (needs footage). Start with Christina's question (script ready: `posts/2026-11-07-christina-reply-script.md`), then one comment a week from the 10/29 post (17 comments). It's the only format built from what the audience asked.
3. **Night review** (needs footage). Tim marks up the day's chart at night after his son's bedtime (confirmed 11/5). A 45-60 s screen recording: the levels, what he took, what he skipped. Dad + process in one clip. No P&L on screen.
4. **Prop firm math** (no footage needed). How a 4-pt ES stop fits a $2,000 drawdown: 2 ES = 5 stops, 1 ES = 10, MES = 20+. A clean graphic and the plain numbers. Education, MES framed as general advice, never as what Tim trades. Ties to the new eval.
5. **"The rule I made after losing 2 accounts"** (no footage needed). Story: trading London cost him 2 accounts, so now it's New York only, nothing before 10 AM. No dollar amounts. A loss story with a lesson, the strongest-performing story type.
6. **Stop placement on the real chart** (footage optional). Today's (or an approved) chart with the 3.5-4 pt stop drawn in: where it sits and why. One frame, 3 captions.
7. **Eval rebuild diary** (needs footage + Tim's go). Day-by-day notes from the new 50K eval: rules followed, trades taken or passed. Balances and P&L make these trade posts, so each needs a go. Weekly, on Friday with the recap.
8. **"One rule, one week"** (no footage needed). Monday names one of Tim's rules (1-2 trades then done, 4-pt hard stop, wait for the label, NY only); Friday says how the week went with it, no numbers. A light series hook ("week 2 of...").

### What a "why I passed" clip is (for Tim)
A screen recording of a setup you watched and did **not** take, with your reason. Example: price sweeps yesterday's low at 9:50, then rips. You didn't take it because it was before 10 AM, or because the stop would have been 8 points. Record 30-60 seconds of the chart (phone screen recording is fine): the setup forming, then where you'd have entered and where the stop would have gone. Then send a one-line reason in the DRT Phone folder or in chat ("passed: before 10 AM"). A voice memo with the reason is even better; Jarvis adds captions and music. Since nothing was traded and there's no P&L, these post without a separate go. Account names, balances and order panels are covered before posting.
