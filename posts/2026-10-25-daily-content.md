## Facebook 9:00 AM (50185, page 993318963868124)

```
1 losing trade doesn't mean your strategy is broken.

Most traders judge a system by its last result. A stop gets hit on Monday, and by Tuesday they're shopping for a new setup, a new indicator, a new mentor.

A single trade can't tell you much. Every setup loses some of the time. That's the reason the stop exists in the first place.

I judge a batch of trades instead, all taken with the same checklist and the risk set before entry. Then I ask 2 things: did I follow the plan on every one, and what do the results say together?

If the rules held and the batch is bad, I adjust. If I broke the rules, the strategy never got a fair test.

Save this for the day a stop-out makes you want to start over.
```
