**Bias Check Mon 11/23: I Leaned Short, Then Took a Long on Paper**

This morning I posted my bias for the day: bearish. London broke the Asia high, and I was looking for price to come back down into the Asia range. First target the Asia low near 7,052, then 6,898.

I also wrote down what would change my mind: price holding above the London high, about 7,139. If that happened, the push up had strength, 7,200 and 7,363.75 opened up, and I'd stand aside on shorts.

**What price did**
ES went the other way. Instead of coming back to the Asia range, it pushed above the London high, cleared 7,200 and was trading near 7,268 by 10:15 AM. My bearish read was wrong.

**The trade (paper account)**

- Sell limit at 7,282.00

**Being honest about it**
This trade went against my morning bias. The bias post already said that above the London high I stand aside on shorts. Price was far above it, and the trade I took was a long.

**The lesson**
Bias is a map, not a trade. Write the map, write what changes your mind, then let price confirm it. Today price picked the other side, and the plan had a line for that.
