ES trades nearly 24 hours a day. Here's the session map, in Eastern time. Asia runs from 6 PM to about 3 AM. London runs from 3 AM to 9:30 AM. New York, the regular session, runs from 9:30 AM to 4 PM. That's my focus. Every day there's a 1 hour break, from 5 to 6 PM. The week runs Sunday 6 PM to Friday 5 PM. Each session can set its own high and low. Know which one you're trading in. Define your risk. Wait for your setup.
