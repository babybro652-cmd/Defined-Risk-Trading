A steady paycheck is the best tool I have in the market.

My week runs Monday through Friday at a distribution center, clocking in at 6 AM and out at 4:30 PM. I train people on order picker skills, and I've traveled to sister sites to train their teams. I'm still there, and I'm good with that.

Online, people treat the day job like a cage. Every other video sells the day you quit. Mine hands me something a lot of people chasing the market never get: my bills are covered before I open a chart. No single day in the market has to pay the rent, so I don't need to force anything.

It also puts a wall around my screen time. No phones on the warehouse floor. Once my lunch break is over, I'm off my phone for the rest of the shift, and nobody has to tell me to step back. At home I've got a baby boy, and chart time comes at night, after he falls asleep.

The W-2 isn't holding me back. It's the ground I'm building on. I'd rather grow slow on solid footing than fast under pressure.
