I've read the market wrong more than once this month, and I'll read it wrong again.

When it happens, the hard stop does its job. It sits 3.5 to 4 points from my entry price, so the loss is a number I agreed to before I clicked. Then the trade is over, and I go back to waiting for my setup to show again.

The market humbles everyone who sticks around long enough. What keeps me in it is the routine: a full-time job during the day, the chart around it, and a review at night of every trade I took, the green ones and the red ones.

Nobody claps for the day after a loss. You log in, look at the chart, and follow the same plan you had before the stop hit. That's the part that builds a trader.
