The first thing my son inherits from me won't be money. It'll be how I handle it.

He's not even a year old. He has no idea what a stop loss is. He'll grow up watching how his dad treats risk, and kids copy what they see long before they understand it.

So I keep my rules boring on purpose. I know my stop before I enter. I know the most I'm willing to lose in a day, and I'm done when I hit it. After 1 or 2 good trades, I close the platform and go pick him up.

People talk about generational wealth like it's a number in an account. I think it starts as habits someone can hand down. An account can be drained in a week. A habit of protecting what you have outlasts any balance.

I'm building both. The habits come first, because without them the money doesn't stay.

Share this with a parent who's building something for their kids.
