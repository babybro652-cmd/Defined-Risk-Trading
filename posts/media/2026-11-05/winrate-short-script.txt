A 40% win rate can still come out ahead. Most traders think they need to win most of their trades. Here's the math. 10 trades. 4 wins at 2R. 6 losses at 1R. Plus 8R, minus 6R, is plus 2R, not counting fees. 1R is what you risk on the trade. With a 4-point stop, 1R is 4 points. So keep your losses the size you planned. Your wins carry the rest. Define your risk. Wait for your setup.
