Wait for the candle to close before you enter. Mid bar, a setup can look perfect. By the close, it can be gone. A wick pokes through your level, then the bar closes back inside, and the early entries are stuck. My rule: no signal on a closed candle, no trade. Missing a move costs nothing. Forcing one can cost a lot. Define your risk. Wait for your setup.
