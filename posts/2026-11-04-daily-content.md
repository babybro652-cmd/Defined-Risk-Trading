I used to enter before my signal finished printing.

I'd watch a setup take shape on the chart and jump in early, sure I was getting a better price. Some of those setups never finished forming. Price turned the other way, and I'd chase the next move to make up for it.

Now the rule is plain: no signal on a closed candle, no trade. If the bar closes and the setup is there, I take it with my stop already set. If it falls apart before the close, waiting cost me nothing.

I miss some moves this way. I'd rather miss one than force one, and my plan stays intact.
