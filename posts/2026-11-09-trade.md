Monday 11/9 Recap: Faded the Rally, Closed When the Break Ended

POST
Two paper trades to start the week, both green.

Sunday night, Asia open:
- Long 2 ES at 7,398.50, out at 7,409.75. +11.25 points a contract, +$1,125.
- Honest note: this is outside my New York window. It worked, and I still don't want it to become a habit.

Monday, New York:
- ES rallied hard into a high. I shorted 2 at 7,560.25 with a 4 point stop at 7,564.25 and a target at 7,534.
- Best point was about +$2,125 open. I covered at 7,544.50 for +15.75 points a contract, +$1,575.
- Why not the target? My lunch break ended. When the break is over, so is the trade.

Total: +$2,700 before fees, paper.

What I'm taking from it:
- Fading a stretched move works best with the stop placed before you click.
- Taking 60% of the move on time beats holding past a rule for 100%.
- A rule about when you stop looking is still a rule.

Video is in the post.

Do you have a hard stop on screen time? What happens to your trades when you ignore it?

Define your risk, wait for your setup.
