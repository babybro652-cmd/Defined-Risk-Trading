## 2
"Paper trading isn't real trading."

Cool. Neither is gambling your rent on a setup that isn't there.

Paper is where I get the reps when the market isn't mine. Same rules. Same stop. Same patience. The only thing missing is the damage.

Ego blows more accounts than bad charts ever will. Keep laughing. I'll keep my capital.

## 3
Some of y'all look down on paper trading but can't sit through one choppy morning without clicking buttons.

That's the whole point. Paper isn't only practice for the skill. It's practice for the emotions. Sitting still when nothing lines up is a skill, and it takes reps.

I'd rather paper trade a bad day than blow a good account trying to force it.

Define your risk. Wait for your setup. If it's not there, it's not there.
