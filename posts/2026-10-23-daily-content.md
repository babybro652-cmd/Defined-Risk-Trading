```
Some of my best trading days end with 0 trades.

I open the ES chart for the New York session with a plan: the levels I care about, the setup I'm waiting for, and a stop of 3.5 to 4 points. If the setup never shows, I don't take a trade. The chart doesn't owe me one.

That used to feel like a wasted morning. Now I see what it protected: my account, my rules, and a clear head for the next session.

A forced trade costs more than the stop. It teaches you that boredom is a reason to click, and you'll repeat that lesson on a day it hurts.

So I grade the day on one question: did I stick to the plan? A 0-trade day where I did is a good day.

Save this for the next slow session.
```
