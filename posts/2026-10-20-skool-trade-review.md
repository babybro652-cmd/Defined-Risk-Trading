TITLE
Trade Review: ES Long, Tuesday 10/20 (Paper)

POST
Trade review. 4 points of risk, 30 points of reward, and a stop that never moved. Here's the full breakdown, with the video below.

THE PLAN
• Instrument: ES, New York session, paper account
• Entry: 7,714.50
• Stop: 7,710.50 (4 points, a hard stop)
• Target: 7,744.50 (30 points)
• Why this trade: AMD set it up. London swept the Asia low, so I was looking for buys. CRT confirmed my entry.
• Why 7,744.50: it was the next target on the volume profile, the low of the next section of pulled orders.

Risk came first. The stop and the dollar amount were set before the order went in. The target only made sense because the loss was small and known.

POSITION SIZE AND $ RISK
• 2 contracts = $100 per point
• 4 point stop x $100 = $400 at risk
• That's Option B from Module 5: 2 ES, a 3.5 to 4 point hard stop, $350 to $400 per trade. One loss at this size ends the session. I knew that number before I clicked buy.

TRADE MANAGEMENT
• The recording picks up at 9:55 AM ET with the trade already up $1,850
• The stop stayed at 7,710.50 the whole time
• No quick 5. Price sat 20+ points in profit for a few minutes, and the plan was the target, so the order stayed where it was.
• Target filled: 2 @ 7,744.50, about 9:58 AM ET

THE RESULT
• +30 points per contract
• 7.5R (30 point move on a 4 point stop)
• +$3,000 before commissions
• Paper account

WHAT WENT RIGHT
✅ Risk defined before entry: stop, size and dollar amount all set first
✅ Stop inside the 3.5 to 4 point rule
✅ Size matched the plan: 2 contracts, $400 max loss
✅ Stop never moved, target never shrank
✅ Let the trade reach the level instead of grabbing a quick 5

WHAT TO WATCH FOR
⚠️ 7.5R is not the normal trade. Most of the time the target is the session high or low, measured in R against your stop. 2R is 7 to 8 points. Don't judge your next trade against this one.
⚠️ The time window. The fill came at 9:58, so this entry was before 10 AM. While you're learning, your window is 10 AM to 12 PM.
⚠️ It's a paper trade. Paper proves the process. It doesn't prove how you'll handle the same order with real money on the line.
⚠️ The exciting part of this trade was the boring part: leaving it alone. If you'd have moved your stop to break-even or closed at +10, be honest about that.

THE LESSON
The stop is the one part of a trade you fully control. Set it, size off it, and leave it alone. A 30 point winner only happens if the 4 point loser was already accepted.
