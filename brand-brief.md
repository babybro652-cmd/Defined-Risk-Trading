# Brand Brief

> Captured: 2026-09-07
> Update this file as the business evolves.

## Business
Defined Risk Trading — a futures trading (ES) education and mentorship program run by Tim Williams. Teaches a structured, repeatable process: AMD (Accumulation/Manipulation/Distribution) framework, CRT (Candle Range Theory) 3-candle entries, Kill Zones, HTF daily bias, session liquidity, and volume profile confluence. Delivered as a curriculum (Modules 1-5 + a required Paper Trading track) with live trade review sessions, not just pre-recorded videos.

## Customer
Retail futures traders serious enough to invest in structured mentorship rather than consume free content. They've been trading undisciplined or unprofitably, and want a defined-risk framework, real accountability, and a live cohort/community to work through it with — not another strategy PDF they'll skim once.

## Primary CTA
Follow the account (audience growth first; monetization comes later).

## Strong Opinion / Wedge
Most trading "gurus" skip risk management entirely. They sell entries and strategies but never teach position sizing or daily loss limits, so students blow up anyway even when the setup was right. Defined Risk Trading requires 20 paper trades over a minimum of 2 weeks, every one following the full pre-trade checklist with risk defined before entry, before touching real money.

## Story Vault
- Just finalized the brand identity: a clean wordmark ("DEFINED RISK TRADING") in near-black, muted navy, and reversed-on-navy versions, a framed wordmark lockup, and a matching DR monogram/favicon set.
- The paper trading bar: 20 trades minimum, 2 weeks minimum, 1-2 trades/day max, every one following the full checklist with defined risk. Not about being profitable yet, about proving the process holds without skipping steps.
- Real risk rules used in the program: never risk more than $1,000 total in a day, no more than $200 during the manipulation session (3:00-9:29 AM ET).

## Voice
Calm confidence, mentor energy with blunt honesty. Short sentences, clean structure, punchy emphasis. No hype or profit talk, process over outcome. Matches the new navy/near-black brand look: professional and clean, expert and authoritative, but not arrogant.

## Universal Voice Rules (apply to every post)
- Contractions always ("don't" not "do not")
- Active voice, short sentences
- Address the reader as "you"
- Numbers as digits ("3 tips" not "three tips")
- No em dashes — use commas, periods, or "..."
- One concrete idea per post, not three
- Specific details over generic statements
