## 1. Refund policy

```
Refund policy

Because our products are digital files you can download right away, all sales are final once a file has been downloaded. We can't take a download back, so we don't offer refunds or exchanges after that point.

We will always fix a file problem. If a file won't open, won't download, prints wrong, or something is missing, reply to your order email and we'll send a working file or help you sort it out. If we can't fix it, we'll refund you.

If you bought the same item twice by mistake, or have not downloaded your order yet, write to us within 14 days and we'll look at it.
```

## 2. Digital delivery

```
Digital delivery

Everything in this shop is a digital download. Nothing is mailed to you.

Your download link appears on screen right after checkout and is emailed to you. If you can't find the email, check your spam or promotions folder, or reply to us and we'll resend it.

Every printable comes in US Letter (US and Canada) and A4 (most other countries). Bundles come as zip files: on an iPhone, tap the zip in the Files app; on Android, open it in Files by Google; on a computer, double-click it.

Print at home or at a local print shop. Colors may look a little different on paper than on your screen.
```

## 3. Terms of use

```
Terms of use

Personal use. When you buy from Peace by Page, you get a personal-use license. You may print as many copies as you need for yourself and your household, at home or at a print shop, and you may use the files in a note-taking or coloring app on your own devices.

Please don't share, resell, upload, or give away the files, and please don't sell printed or colored copies. The designs and text remain the property of Peace by Page.

Counselors, teachers and groups. Using our printables with clients, students, a class or a support group needs a separate counselor or group license. Write to us and we'll set it up.

Our products are for general wellbeing and self-reflection. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. By buying, you agree that you use them at your own discretion.
```

## 4. Privacy note

```
Privacy

We collect only what we need to deliver your order and help you with it: your name, email address and order details. Payments are handled by Payhip and its payment processors (Stripe or PayPal). We never see or store your card number.

We use your email to send your download and to answer your messages. If you join our email list, we'll send occasional notes about new printables, and you can unsubscribe any time with one click.

We never sell or rent your information. Payhip's own privacy policy also applies to purchases made through this store.

Questions or a request to delete your data? Reply to any email from us.
```

## 5. Disclaimer

```
Please note

Peace by Page journals and printables are self-reflection tools for general wellbeing. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and they support, not replace, care from a licensed professional. If something in a journal doesn't feel right for you, skip it. If anxiety is getting in the way of daily life, please talk with a doctor or a licensed mental health professional.
```

## 6. Crisis resources

```
If you need help now

In the US: call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline), free and confidential, 24/7. You can also text HOME to 741741.

In an emergency: call 911 if you or someone else is in danger.

Outside the US: call your local emergency number, or find a free helpline in your country at findahelpline.com.
```
