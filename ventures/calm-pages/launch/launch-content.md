### Email 2 (day 3): how to use worry time

**Subject:** A 15-minute appointment for your worries
**Preview text:** One small habit from the full journal you can try today.

Hi [FIRST NAME],

How are the first few pages going? Whether you have filled in three days or none, you are welcome here.

Today, one tool from the full journal: **worry time**. It is a common CBT-based exercise, and it works like this:

1. **Pick a time.** About 15 minutes, at the same time each day. Early evening works for a lot of people. Try not to put it right before bed.
2. **When a worry shows up outside that time, write it down.** One line is enough. Then tell yourself, "Not now, later."
3. **When worry time comes, read the list.** Some worries will have shrunk or gone on their own. For the ones still there, ask: is this a real problem I can do something about?
4. **If yes, choose one small step** and when you'll do it. **If no,** name it ("there's a what-if") and let it pass for today.

It can feel strange at first. Most people find it gets easier with practice, so give it a week before you decide.

The full 90-day journal has a worry time page, a worry tree and a quick thought record in its SOS Toolkit, ready to print as often as you need.

Take care,
