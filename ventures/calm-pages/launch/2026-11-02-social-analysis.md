### Best and worst
- Best: **V3 coloring reveal on TikTok, 89 views, 1 like.** Coloring is a native TikTok niche (coloring and journaling videos get pushed to people who watch them), and it is the only reel whose subject is an activity, not a product.
- Second: V4 three editions on TikTok (27) and on IG (13).
- Worst: V3 on Instagram (6 views, 5 reach). Same video that won on TikTok, so the problem on IG is distribution for a brand-new account, not the topic.
- Facebook: 0 likes and 0 comments on all 6 posts, 0 views on the launch repost. The page has no audience yet and Blotato can't read reel views there.

### Why the numbers are this low
1. **People leave in the first second.** IG average watch time is 0.88 to 1.11 s on 15 to 17 s reels, so about 5 to 7% of each reel gets watched. The hook is a text card on a flat PDF page that fades in, which reads as an ad.
2. **The reels are silent.** The files have no audio. By hand Tim was meant to add a soft track; Blotato posts them as they are. Silent reels get less push on TikTok and IG, and there is no sound to stop the scroll.
3. **Every reel is a product pitch.** Price, "link in bio" and edition names in each one. A new account with no followers gets shown to strangers, and strangers swipe past ads.
4. **Brand-new accounts.** IG and TikTok were connected 10/26, so the algorithm has almost no data on who to show them to. TikTok gives new accounts a small test pool (that is where the 89 came from); IG gives almost none to non-followers.
5. Captions and hashtags are fine and compliant (support note and 988 on each). They are long, but captions don't drive reach on a reel nobody watches past second one. Hashtags are broad (#selfcare, #journaling); search terms in the on-screen text would help more.
6. Posting time: too few posts to judge. V2 at 8:45 PM did about the same as V4 at 6:30 PM on IG.

## 3. DRT lesson: real footage beats text cards

DRT's 10/30 real-chart reel got 2,028 IG views; its text-card reels got 15 to 69. Every Peace by Page reel so far is the text-card kind (rendered PDF pages plus captions), and the IG numbers (6 to 13) sit right where DRT's text cards did.

The Peace by Page version of "real chart footage" is **real paper and real hands**:
- A pencil coloring a printed page, filmed from above, with the real scratching sound.
- Page flips through a printed journal in a binder (ASMR sound, no music needed).
- A hand writing on the Day 1 page: three good things, one worry, then the kinder rewrite.
- A finger tracing the box-breathing square on paper while the count shows on screen.
- The printer pushing out the free sampler pages.

These are also the formats the journaling and coloring corners of TikTok already reward, and they show the product without pitching it. No face or voice needed.

## 4. Recommendations

### Top 5 changes
1. **Switch to real paper and hands.** Print one journal and the coloring pack at home (Calm Petals first, it won on TikTok), and film 3 short clips a week on the phone. Keep the rendered reels only as backup.
2. **Make TikTok the main platform, Pinterest second.** TikTok gave 2 to 15 times IG's views with no followers. Pinterest is where printables get found by search and it links straight to Payhip. Instagram gets the same videos (cross-post) with no extra work. Facebook stays on autopilot with no new effort.
3. **Hook in the first second, with sound.** Start on motion (pencil already moving, page already turning), put a 3 to 6 word line on screen at 0.0 s, and keep the real sound (scratching, page flips). Keep clips 7 to 12 s so they loop. Examples: "Color with me for 10 seconds", "What I write when my brain won't stop", "Day 1 of a 90-day anxiety journal".
4. **Lead with value, not the product.** Most posts should be the activity (color with me, a prompt, a breathing reset). Mention the shop on 1 post in 3. The free 7-day sampler is the call to action, not the $11.99 journal. On TikTok, say "free sampler: payhip.com/PeaceByPage" on screen in the last second, since the link isn't clickable.
5. **TikTok habits.** Post TikTok by hand from the phone app when Tim films (so he can add a trending quiet sound and use TikTok's own text), reply to any comment, use search words in the on-screen text ("anxiety journal", "printable journal", "coloring for adults"), and try one photo carousel a week (TikTok photo mode: 5 to 7 shots of real pages). Cadence: 4 to 5 TikToks a week beats 7 silent ones.
