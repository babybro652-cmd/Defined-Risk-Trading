# Calm Wings: Market Research and Launch Plan

Prepared 10/2/2026 by the Ventures agent for Tim. Research only: no accounts, listings or posts were created.

**Product:** Calm Wings, a 168-page, US Letter, 90-day butterfly-themed anxiety journal. Start Here (6 pp), SOS Toolkit (10 pp), Days 1-90 (one page each, undated: "Day X of 90" plus a blank date line), weekly check-ins with a reward coloring page after every 7 days, monthly pages (mood butterfly, habit tracker, kind letter), Building Confidence (worry time and similar tools, 8 pp), Look Back (4 pp), and a back page with "supports, not replaces, care from a professional" and 988. A generic edition with no personal name already exists.

**How to read the figures:** **[V]** = verified, with the source linked. **[S]** = taken from a search-result snippet or a third-party site; I could not open the original page. **[E]** = my estimate, with the reasoning shown.

**What I could not verify:** Amazon product pages returned a captcha or HTTP 500, and Etsy returned HTTP 403. That means I have no Amazon Best Sellers Rank, no live Amazon prices, and no Etsy sales counts, and every Amazon review count below is a snippet [S]. Google Trends data came from the Trends endpoint through `pytrends` (US, past 5 years, pulled 10/2/2026), so the index values are real [V]. They are relative (0-100), not search volumes.

---

## Verdict (short)

The product is worth selling, as a **low-cost side test, not an income plan**. Demand for anxiety and mental health journals is steady and well established. Competition is heavy at the low end on Etsy and among the credentialed authors on Amazon. Calm Wings has a real edge: it is longer, it follows a structured 90-day plan with weekly rewards, and its evidence-based tools, butterfly coloring pages and theme all hang together. It lacks reviews, a credentialed author and a brand.

**First channel:** Etsy, as an instant PDF download (the file is about 90% ready). **Second:** an Amazon KDP paperback with a black-and-white interior and a color cover, live before the November-December gift peak. Payhip, Stan Store, TPT, a separate Shopify store and local sales can wait.

What this means:
- **Self-buyers** search in January to April (New Year reset) and in September (back to routine, back to school). "Anxiety journal" and "CBT journal" follow this pattern.

- **May (Mental Health Awareness Month) is a search low, not a peak.** Awareness months are good hooks for posts, not demand peaks. World Mental Health Day (10/10) and Stress Awareness Month (April) work the same way.
- **Timing now:** today is 10/2. Listing on Etsy by mid-October and on KDP by early November catches the biggest window of the year.

### What makes the top sellers sell
1. **Credentials on the cover** (PhD, LCSW, LCAT). This is the biggest trust signal for therapists and parents.
2. **A clear method in the title**: "Guided CBT Journal with Prompts".
3. **Small daily effort**: "5 minutes a day", one page a day.
4. **A premium, giftable object**: hardcover, linen, gold foil, thick paper (Clever Fox, Four Progress).
5. **A discreet cover**: Worry for Nothing has no anxiety wording on the outside. "Calm Wings" already works this way, which is a plus.
6. **Review volume**: hundreds to thousands of reviews. A new listing starts at zero, which is the hardest gap to close.
7. **On Etsy, perpetual sales and bundles**: "100+ pages", "mega bundle", GoodNotes-ready, prices of $3-15.

### Gaps Calm Wings fills
- **A journal-plus-coloring combination is not new.** Comparables 7 and 8 already do it. Don't lead with that alone.
- **A complete 90-day plan** is the real difference: daily page, weekly check-in, "I handled it" log, reward coloring page, then monthly mood butterfly, habit tracker and kind letter, ending in a Look Back. Most Etsy PDFs are 16-111 loose pages with no plan.
- **A range of evidence-based tools in one book**: thought records, worry time, grounding, expressive writing, positive-affect journaling and an SOS toolkit with crisis resources. The KDP-style coloring hybrids are mostly affirmations and mandalas.
- **One consistent butterfly theme**, with original vector art and coloring as a weekly reward rather than filler.
- **168 pages, at the top of the range** for printables.
- **Gaps that remain:** no credentialed reviewer, no tablet (GoodNotes) version, no hardcover, no reviews.

## 4. Best format and pricing

### 4.1 Format comparison

| Format | Effort to make | Buyer appeal | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Printable PDF** (Letter) | Done, apart from small fixes | Instant, cheap, prints at home; 168 pages costs the buyer ink and paper | **Launch now** |

### 4.2 KDP print cost and royalty (8.5 x 11 is a "large trim" size)

Official US print costs for large trim [V, [KDP printing costs](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340)]:
- Black ink, 110+ pages: $1.00 + $0.017 per page, so **168 pp = $3.86**
- Standard color: $1.00 + $0.0402 per page, so **168 pp = $7.75**
- Premium color: $1.00 + $0.08 per page, so **168 pp = $14.44** (the minimum list price for 60% royalty would be about $24.07)

| Interior | List price | Royalty per sale (60%, Amazon.com) | Expanded distribution (40%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| B&W, 168 pp | $12.99 | $3.94 | $1.34 |
| **B&W, 168 pp** | **$14.99** | **$5.14** | $2.14 |
| B&W, 168 pp | $16.99 | $6.34 | $2.94 |
| B&W, about 186 pp (blank backs behind coloring pages) | $14.99 | $4.83 | n/a |
| Standard color, 168 pp | $19.99 | $4.24 | n/a |
| Standard color, 168 pp | $24.99 | $7.24 | n/a |
| Premium color, 168 pp | $29.99 | $3.55 | n/a |

[E] All royalty math is royalty = list x rate - print cost, using KDP's formula and the costs above.

**B&W interior with a color cover is clearly the better choice.** The interior is line art with light pastel accents, and coloring pages are meant to be black and white. A color interior would double the print cost, push the price above the $15-20 range comparables sell at, and earn less per copy. Check the pastel accents in grayscale; some may print as muddy gray and should become line-only.

**Recommended KDP price: $14.99.** That is below the branded hardcovers ($18-30) and the credentialed paperbacks ($16.95-21.95), and in line with gift journals ($14.99). It returns $5.14 per copy. Price below $9.99 only for a deliberate promotion, since the 50% royalty cuts the margin to almost nothing.

## 6. Legal and ethics guardrails

### 6.1 Claims
- **Never say:** treats, cures, heals or reduces anxiety; therapy replacement; "clinically proven"; "anxiety relief" as a promise; "CBT therapy".
- **Fine:** "supports calm daily reflection", "CBT-inspired prompts", "a gentle 90-day habit", "for anyone who worries".
- Etsy bans claims that a product "prevents, heals, treats, diagnoses, mitigates, or cures" a condition, and **"adding a disclaimer does not make a prohibited medical claim acceptable."** [V, [Etsy Medical Claims policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/medical-claims/239976157654) via search summary] Amazon removes listings for unsupported medical claims in titles, descriptions, A+ content and covers [S, [Traverse Legal](https://www.traverselegal.com/blog/navigating-amazon-fda-and-ftc-compliance/)].

### 6.2 Disclaimer (inside the book, listing description and copyright page)
> Calm Wings is a self-reflection journal for general wellbeing. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it supports, not replaces, care from a licensed professional. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988 or text HOME to 741741. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

### 6.3 Platform policies
- **Etsy AI disclosure:** since June 2025 the Creativity Standards cover digital downloads, and listings made "with the use of AI" must say so in the description [S, [Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/) and seller guides]. The art is code-generated, but the code and the wording were written with an AI assistant. **Recommendation: disclose honestly**, for example "Designed by [shop] using AI-assisted tools; all art is original." List it as "Designed by a seller".
- **KDP AI disclosure:** KDP asks privately at publishing whether the text or images were AI-generated; this is not shown to buyers [S, [KDP AI policy summaries](https://www.builtwritten.com/blog/disclose-ai-in-your-book-kdp-policy-2026)]. The text in `content.py` was drafted with AI, so **answer yes for text and images** unless Tim rewrites it himself.
- **KDP low-content rules:** prompt journals can count as low-content, which means no free ISBN and no Look Inside [V]. Choose "not low-content" only if KDP agrees the instructional sections qualify.
- **KDP duplicates:** don't publish many near-identical versions. KDP limits new titles to 10 per format per week and watches for "minimally differentiated" books [S, [PubNook](https://pubnook.com/article/amazon-kdp-low-content-book-policy-2026-whats-allowed-and-what-gets-removed)].

### 6.4 Copyright
- CBT techniques (thought records, worry time, grounding, 5-4-3-2-1) are methods, and methods can't be copyrighted. Specific worksheet text and layouts can be.
- Don't copy Therapist Aid worksheets (their terms bar reselling) or pages from *Mind Over Mood* and similar books. Calm Wings' wording lives in `content.py` and should stay original. **Before launch, have an agent check for phrases that closely match well-known worksheets.**
- Coloring art is original vector code (`butterfly_art.py`, `designs.py`). Don't add stock or downloaded clip art without a commercial license.
- Quotes on daily pages: make sure they are original lines, not quotes from famous people or copyrighted sources.

### 6.5 Trademark check: "Calm Wings"
- **USPTO:** a live search of the USPTO trademark database (tmsearch.uspto.gov API, 10/2/2026) found **0 records for "CALM WINGS"**, and 0 for "calmwings", "calm wing", "calming wings" and "wings of calm". [V] The search worked: it returned 2,857 results for "calm".
- **Main risk to note:** Calm.com, Inc. (the Calm app) owns **CALM, Reg. No. 6337486**, which covers Class 16 "books, ... exercise books, activity books, educational books, in the field of meditation and mindfulness; stationery", plus DAILY CALM and others. [V, USPTO record] Calm.com protects its brand. On the other hand, CALM is a crowded word in Class 16, with live marks such as COLOR TO CALM (coloring books), CALMUP (journals), CALM-IN-A-BOOK and CALM MIND EXERCISES. That suggests compound names coexist with it. [V]
- **On Amazon there are already two near-identical titles:**
  - *Calm Wings: A Mindful Bird Coloring Book for Adults* (Keith CA, 103 pp, 8.5x11, 8/2025). [S, [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Calm-Wings-Mindful-Coloring-Adults/dp/B0FLVKW578)]
  - *Calming Wings: A Butterfly Coloring Book for Relaxation and Mindfulness* (Bonnie Wilczynski, LCAT, 90 pp, 11/2025). [S, [Amazon](https://us.amazon.com/Calming-Wings-Butterfly-Relaxation-Mindfulness/dp/B0G2512N7T)]
  - Single book titles generally can't be trademarked, so this is a findability and confusion problem rather than a legal one.
- **Etsy:** I couldn't search it because Etsy blocks bots. Tim should search "Calm Wings" on Etsy before naming the shop.
- **My read (not legal advice):** risk is low for using "Calm Wings" as a product title. Avoid any look like the Calm app (no blue gradient, no script "Calm" logo). Always pair the name with "90-Day Anxiety Journal" so it can't be mistaken for the bird or butterfly coloring books. **Don't file a trademark now** (USPTO fees run a few hundred dollars per class [E]). If the brand earns real money, pay for an attorney clearance search first.

### Realistic first-90-day expectations [E]
- **Traffic:** a new Etsy listing with no reviews, plus 1 pin a day, plausibly gets about 200-600 listing views in 90 days. That is a guess, not data.
- **Conversion:** new digital shops typically convert 1-2%, rising toward 5-10% once established [S, [Merchize](https://merchize.com/what-is-a-good-conversion-rate-on-etsy/), [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/what-is-a-good-conversion-rate-on-etsy/)].
- **Etsy result:** about **2-12 sales in 90 days**, or roughly **$20-$130** net. Zero is possible. A Pinterest pin that takes off, or a holiday gift-guide mention, could do better.
- **KDP:** without ads, **0-10 copies** over the holidays, or **$0-$50**. KDP ads could help but cost money and need Tim's approval; don't start them before there are a few reviews.
- **Biggest unknowns:** whether Pinterest catches on, whether reviews come in, and whether the 2026 journaling spike returns in January.
- **Decision point (about 1/15/2027):** with 10+ Etsy sales or 2+ reviews, add the tablet version and test a small Etsy Ads budget (with approval). With under 3 sales, keep it as a passive listing and spend no more time on it.
