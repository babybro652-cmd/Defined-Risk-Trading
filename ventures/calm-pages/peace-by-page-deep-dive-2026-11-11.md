## 3. Funnel: post to product page to purchase

Roughly: ~1,500 views over 3 weeks > unknown profile visits > unknown store visits > 0 sampler downloads > 0 orders.

Where it leaks, biggest first:
1. **Reach is too small to sell anything.** At typical rates (1 to 2% of viewers visit a profile, a few percent of those buy) 1,500 views predicts 0 orders. The result is about what the math says, not a broken store.

FB is the only platform with a clickable product link per post, and FB views are low.

## 4. Market check (brief)

Printable anxiety journals sell at $2 to $27 on Payhip, Gumroad and Selar; most sit at $3 to $9, with 90-day versions at about $6 to $15 and bundles at $12 to $27 ([Payhip examples](https://payhip.com/b/McbW8), [Journaling For Battling Anxiety 90-day, $14.49](https://journalingfor.gumroad.com/l/battlinganxiety-x90-a5), [Calm Within 90-day](https://selar.com/s049475j17), [Getly 90-day, $6.99](https://www.getly.store/product/anxiety-mental-health-journal), [Calm Compass CBT, $27](https://thecalmcompass.gumroad.com/l/zmdjrd/landing/embed)). No sales ranks were available.

- **Price is fine.** $11.99 for a 169-page 90-day journal is mid-market; $16.99 for the bundle is fair. Price is not the problem with 0 visitors. No change recommended until there is traffic to test against.
- **Positioning edge holds:** full 90-day plan + evidence-based tools + matching coloring art in 3 themes, Letter and A4. Few competitors combine journal and coloring (the 10/2 research found the same).
- **Gaps vs top sellers:** no reviews, no credentialed name, no tablet/fillable version, no paperback.
- **Timing:** Nov to Dec is the gift peak for guided journals and adult coloring (10/2 Trends work: guided journal +66% in December, adult coloring +84%). Jan to Apr is the self-buyer peak. The next 7 weeks are the best window of the year.
