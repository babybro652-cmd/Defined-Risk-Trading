# KDP setup: Calm Wings paperback (Tim, 11/12)

Phone-friendly, but do steps 3 to 6 on a computer: KDP's upload and previewer work best there.
Time: about 45 minutes total. Cost: only the proof copies (about $3.04 each to print + shipping + tax).

## The 2 files to upload
Both are built and in the repo (not in Drive yet; the Drive connector can't upload big PDFs):
- **Interior:** `ventures/calm-pages/kdp/out/calm-wings-kdp-interior.pdf` (170 pages, 8.5 x 11 in, no bleed, 16 MB)
- **Cover:** `ventures/calm-pages/kdp/out/calm-wings-kdp-cover.pdf` (one-piece wrap 17.63 x 11.25 in, 0.383 in spine, 0.125 in bleed, barcode box left blank for Amazon)

Get them onto your computer first: GitHub (repo Defined-Risk-Trading, branch `claude/custom-jarvis-claude-8t99na`, folder `ventures/calm-pages/kdp/out/`, tap the file > Download), or ask Jarvis to put them somewhere easier.
Don't use the Payhip journal PDF: it has print-at-home pages and the wrong page count for the cover.

## 1. Create the account (5 min)
1. Go to **kdp.amazon.com** > Sign in. Use your normal Amazon login, or "Create account".
2. Accept the terms. You land on the Bookshelf.

## 2. Tax interview (10 min)
1. Top right: **Your Account** > Tax Information > **Complete tax interview**.
2. Individual (not business), US person: yes. This makes it a **W-9**.
3. Tax ID: your **SSN** is the simple choice (sole proprietor). Use an EIN only if you want a business (for example Fantasy Homes LLC) to own the royalties; ask your tax person before mixing it in.
4. Name and address exactly as on your tax return. Sign electronically. Status should read "Complete".

## 3. Bank details (5 min)
1. Your Account > **Getting Paid** > Add a bank account (US).
2. Account holder name, routing number, account number (from a check or your bank app).
3. Royalties pay monthly, about 60 days after the month of a sale.

## 4. Title setup: Create > Paperback
**Details tab**
- Language: English
- Title: **Calm Wings**
- Subtitle: **A 90-Day Anxiety Journal with Daily Prompts, Mood Tracker, Grounding Tools and Butterfly Coloring Pages**
- Series: Peace by Page (optional; you can add it later)
- Author: **Peace by Page** (a pen name is allowed) or your own name
- Description, 7 keywords and 2 categories: copy from `ventures/calm-pages/kdp/README.md` (Description and Keywords sections)
- Categories: Self-Help > Journal Writing; Self-Help > Anxieties & Phobias
- Publishing rights: I own the copyright
- Low-content book: **No**. Large print: No. Adult content: No.
- Save and Continue

**Content tab**
- ISBN: **Get a free KDP ISBN** (can't be changed after publishing; fine for us)
- Publication date: leave blank
- Print options: **Black & white interior**, **White paper**
- Trim size: **8.5 x 11 in**
- Bleed: **No bleed**
- Cover finish: **Matte**
- Manuscript: upload **calm-wings-kdp-interior.pdf**
- Cover: "Upload a cover you already have" > **calm-wings-kdp-cover.pdf**
- AI-generated content: **Yes**. Text: AI-generated with some edits. Images: AI-generated with some edits.
- **Launch Previewer**. Page through all 170 pages. Fix nothing unless it flags an error (send Jarvis a screenshot if it does). Approve.
- Note: the interior file has a few light color accents. On a black and white interior KDP prints them as gray. That is expected; check on the proof that the light lines and the coloring-page outlines are dark enough.
- Save and Continue

**Rights & Pricing tab**
- Territories: All
- Primary marketplace: Amazon.com
- Price: **$12.99** (printing is about $3.04, so about $4.75 royalty per sale)
- Expanded distribution: off for now
- **Do NOT press Publish yet.** Press "Save as Draft".

## 5. Order author proof copies (tomorrow, 5 min)
1. Bookshelf > Calm Wings paperback > the **"..."** button > **Request printed proofs** (also at the bottom of the Content tab).
2. Pick quantity (up to 5 per order) and Amazon.com. Submit.
3. KDP emails a link in about 4 hours. Open it, the proof sits in your Amazon cart: check out like a normal order.
4. Proofs have a "Not for Resale" band on the cover. Shipping usually takes about 1 to 2 weeks; pay for faster shipping if offered, since the December gift window matters.

## 6. Check the proof, then publish
Check: cover colors and spine text centered, nothing cut off at the page edges, gray accents readable, coloring outlines dark, pages in order.
- Looks good: Bookshelf > Edit paperback content > Rights & Pricing > **Publish Your Paperback Book**.
- Something wrong: send Jarvis a photo; Jarvis rebuilds the file, you re-upload, and you can publish without a second proof if the fix is small.
- Review takes up to 72 hours. Then it shows "Live" and the Amazon link comes by email. Send it to Jarvis for the store, Pinterest and posts.

Optional to save a week: publish right after the previewer approves, and use the proof copy as your first check (you can fix and re-upload a live book). Your call.
