# Peace by Page: free 7-day sampler

Lead magnet for payhip.com/PeaceByPage (launch step 34). 14 pages, Calm Wings art, in Letter and A4:
cover, before you begin (disclaimer, 988 and crisis lines, terms), how to use it, days 1 to 7, week 1 review,
1 reward coloring page, a cut-out pocket SOS card (two copies), and a closing page with the three editions,
the store link, the disclaimer and crisis lines.

Rebuild: `python3 launch/build_sampler.py` (from `ventures/calm-pages/`).
