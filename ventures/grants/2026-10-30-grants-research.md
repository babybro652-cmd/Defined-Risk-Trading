# Small-business grants research (10/30/2026)

Research only. Nothing applied for, no accounts created. Prepared for Tim Williams
(Roxboro, Person County, NC). Verify every window on the official page before applying;
many grant listing sites copy old cycles forward.

## Bottom line

- The Instagram reel is **misleading**. All five programs exist (or existed), but none is
  "for male entrepreneurs", the "$200k" is a sum of single prizes from five unrelated
  contests, three of the five have no current cycle, and one (Visa) is a fintech startup
  pitch competition, not a small business grant.
- The best realistic fits for Tim are local and early-stage: Piedmont Community College
  Small Business Center BOOST (Person County), the Freed Fellowship ($500 monthly, rolling),
  and Progressive's commercial vehicle grant for Legionz Detailing when the next cycle opens.
- Most grants need a real business: an entity (LLC), an EIN, a business bank account,
  and ideally some revenue. Registering Legionz (or confirming Fantasy Homes) is the
  single step that unlocks the most options.

## Scam and hype check on the reel

- None of the five is restricted to men. "For male entrepreneurs" is reel framing.
- Each figure is one winner's prize from a large pool, not money available to every applicant.
- Real grants never charge an application fee and never ask for bank login details. If the
  reel links to a "grant finder" that charges a fee, or a DM offers a "guaranteed grant",
  treat it as a scam. Apply only through the program's own site.

### Details

**1. Fast Break for Small Business (LegalZoom + NBA).** A multiyear $6M commitment that
started in 2021. 2024 round: applications closed 9/13/2024 8 PM ET, 150 winners announced
by 9/30/2024. Past eligibility: US-based for-profit, operating at least 3 to 6 months (varied by
year), focus on underrepresented owners. No 2025 or 2026 round found on LegalZoom's
program page or newsroom. Competitive: thousands of applicants for 150 slots.
Sources: [LegalZoom program page](https://www.legalzoom.com/fastbreakforsmallbusiness),
[Accion Opportunity Fund](https://aofund.org/news/fastbreak_with_nba/),
[NBA.com](https://www.nba.com/news/nba-legalzoom-provide-minorities-with-opportunity-to-scale-their-business).

**2. Progressive Driving Small Business Forward.** 2025: $1M, 20 grants of $50,000 for
buying a commercial vehicle and related expenses; plus a 12-week virtual "Boost Camp".
Must be the business's leading executive, 10 or fewer employees, under $5M gross revenue,
legal US resident 18+, show the need for a qualifying commercial vehicle and a plan to scale.
Heavy trucks (over 26,000 lbs) and off-road machinery excluded. Deadline was 6/20/2025.
Earlier rounds: 2022 (Hispanic-owned, $25k), 2023 (Black-owned, $25k), 2024 (deadline 6/14/2024).
Money must go to the vehicle. Very competitive, but the vehicle-need story is a strong
fit for a mobile detailing business. Sources:
[Progressive newsroom, 5/12/2025](https://progressive.mediaroom.com/2025-05-12-progressive-drives-small-business-forward-with-1-million-in-commercial-vehicle-grants),
[PR Newswire 2024 awards](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/progressive-insurance-hello-alice-award-1-million-to-small-business-owners-302242258.html).

**3. Warrior Rising.** Nonprofit for veteran and military-family entrepreneurs (veterans,
active duty, Guard/Reserve, military spouses, immediate family members). Path: complete
Warrior University, then apply to pitch at an invite-only Business Shower; top finalists
get non-dilutive grants of $2,000 to $150,000. Non-veterans with no military family tie do
not qualify. Sources: [Warrior Rising Business Showers](https://warriorrising.org/business-showers/),
[VA News](https://news.va.gov/146863/warrior-university-20k-veteran-business-grants/).

**4. Intuit QuickBooks.** The $10,000 program was a 2024 one-off (20 winners, 5/28 to
6/30/2024, short essay, 3 months mentorship). Current Intuit options: the QuickBooks x
Mailchimp Small Business Hero program ($20k, nomination-based, 2026 phases closed) and
Intuit IDEAS (1-year accelerator, $5,000 cash on completion plus a technology grant, set
metro cohorts plus veteran/military spouse track; 2025 included Charlotte; 2026 apps
reported closed 10/15/2026). Sources:
[Business Wire 5/28/2024](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240528201582/en),
[Intuit IDEAS FAQ](https://www.intuit.com/company/corporate-responsibility/intuit-ideas/faqs/).

**5. Visa Everywhere Initiative.** A global pitch competition for fintech and payments
startups, judged on technology that works with Visa's network. Prizes vary by year and
region (2022 global grand prize $100,000; 2023 global winner $50,000; North America
regional prizes around $10,000). Not a fit: Tim's businesses are not payments technology.
Sources: [Visa press release (2023 finalists)](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.19941.html),
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/sponsor/visa-everywhere-initiative/visa-competition-fuels-startup-growth/).

## Match summary

- **Legionz Detailing** is the best grant vehicle: local, tangible needs (van/truck,
  equipment), fits Progressive, BOOST, Chamber micro-grants, Hello Alice and NAACP.
  Needs: LLC, EIN, business bank account, a one-page plan with costs.
- **Peace by Page / Propa Pit** fit general product-business grants (Freed, FedEx) and
  possibly NC IDEA MICRO once there is revenue.
- **DRT, PL, Fantasy Homes** are poor grant fits (financial education and real estate
  investing are often excluded).

## Top 3 to go after first

1. **PCC Small Business Center BOOST** (Roxboro): local, small pool, startups OK,
   comes with free planning help that strengthens every other application. Call or email
   the center at piedmontcc.edu/sbc.
2. **Freed Fellowship**: rolling, short application, open to any US small business owner.
   Low effort, apply monthly for an operating venture.
3. **Progressive Driving Small Business Forward for Legionz**: biggest realistic prize
   ($50k toward a work vehicle). Not open now; past cycles opened in May. Use the winter to
   register Legionz, get the EIN and write the vehicle-need story so it is ready.
