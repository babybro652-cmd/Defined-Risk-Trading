## 1. Open your Etsy shop (on your phone, about 45 to 60 minutes)

Have ready: your driver's license or state ID, your SSN (for taxes), your bank routing and account numbers, a debit or credit card, and your phone for text codes.

**Before you start (5 minutes)**
- [ ] Search "Calm Wings" on Etsy to see what's already there.
- [ ] Pick the shop name. Rules: 4 to 20 characters, letters and numbers only, no spaces (Etsy capitalizes words for you, e.g. CalmWingsJournal). Ideas from the research: CalmWingsJournal, QuietWingsStudio, GentleWingsPaperCo.

**Know the fees (US, as of the 10/2 research; Etsy shows the current amounts during signup)**
- One-time shop setup fee: about $15, non-refundable, charged when you open (Etsy may waive it in some cases).
- Listing fee: $0.20 per listing, every 4 months or when it sells. 5 listings = $1.00.
- Transaction fee: 6.5% of each sale.
- Payment processing: 3% + $0.25 per order (US).
- Offsite Ads: 15% only on sales that come from Etsy's outside ads. Shops under $10K a year can opt out in Settings > Advertising and Marketing.
- Example: a $11.99 sale nets about $10.40.

**Steps (Etsy app or etsy.com in your phone browser)**
1. [ ] Sign in to Etsy with babybro652@gmail.com (or create an account). Etsy app: tap **You** > **Sell on Etsy**. Browser: go to **etsy.com/sell** and tap **Get started**.
2. [ ] **Shop preferences:** language English, country United States, currency US Dollar. Choose "I sell part-time" if asked.
3. [ ] **Name your shop:** enter the name you picked. If it's taken, Etsy suggests options; check with Jarvis before choosing a very different name.
4. [ ] **Stock your shop:** Etsy makes you create one listing before you can open. Use listing 1 from `listings.md` (the journal). Upload the files from `1-journal/files/` and the 5 images from `1-journal/images/`. You can save it and finish the others after the shop is open.
5. [ ] **How you'll get paid:** choose "Individual / sole owner". Enter your legal name, home address, date of birth and SSN. Add your bank account for deposits (routing + account number).
6. [ ] **Identity verification:** Etsy may ask for a photo of your ID and a selfie. Follow the on-screen camera steps. This can take a few minutes to a few days to approve.
7. [ ] **Set up billing:** add a credit or debit card. This pays the setup fee and listing fees.
8. [ ] **Your shop security:** turn on **two-factor authentication** (Etsy requires it for sellers). Pick "Authenticator app" or "Text message". Save the backup codes in a safe place (a note on your phone is fine).
9. [ ] Tap **Open your shop**. The setup fee is charged here.

**After the shop is open (20 minutes)**
- [ ] Settings > Info & Appearance: add the shop announcement, About section and a shop icon (use `1-journal/images/01_cover.png` cropped, or ask Jarvis for a square icon).
- [ ] Settings > Policies: paste the policies below.
- [ ] Settings > Advertising and Marketing: decide on Offsite Ads (opting out is allowed under $10K a year).
- [ ] Create listings 2 to 5 from `listings.md`. Tip: on listing 1, tap **Copy** to start each new one, then change title, price, files, photos, tags and description.
- [ ] Optional: Marketing > Sales and discounts > a 25% launch sale for 2 weeks.
- [ ] Publish around World Mental Health Day (10/10) if you like the timing.

## 5. Shop FAQ

**Where are my files?**
After checkout: Etsy app > You > Purchases and reviews > Download files. Or open the download link in your Etsy order email. Files can't be downloaded in some in-app browsers; if a download doesn't start, open Etsy in Safari or Chrome.

**Which size should I print?**
US Letter for the US, Canada and Mexico. A4 for the UK, Europe, Australia and most other countries. Every listing includes both.

**How do I print double-sided?**
Choose "Print on both sides" and "Flip on long edge". No two-sided option? Print the odd pages, put the stack back in, then print the even pages. The How to Print guide in each download walks through it.

**Can a print shop print it for me?**
Yes. Email or bring the PDF to a local print or office shop and ask for actual size, 24 or 32 lb paper, and coil binding on the left. The license allows printing for your personal use.

**Can I use it on an iPad or tablet?**
You can import the PDF into GoodNotes, Notability or another PDF app and write on it. The files are designed for printing, so the lines are sized for a pen.

**Are the pages dated?**
No. Everything is undated, so you can start any day of any year.

**Can I share it with a friend?**
Please buy a copy for each person. To give it as a gift, print it and give the printed copy, or buy a copy and send the files to that one person instead of keeping them yourself.

**I'm a counselor or teacher. Can I use these with clients or a class?**
The standard license is personal use. Message me about group use.

**Is this therapy?**
No. Calm Wings is a self-reflection tool that supports, not replaces, care from a professional. If you are struggling, please reach out to a doctor or therapist. In a crisis in the US, call or text 988.

**Something is wrong with my file.**
Message me with the item name and what happened, and I'll send a fix.
