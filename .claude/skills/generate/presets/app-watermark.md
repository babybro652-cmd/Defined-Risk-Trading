# Preset: app-watermark

Pins your product's logo or watermark as a reference so it rides on every frame. This is how a faceless channel doubles as free advertising for your app.

## Locked prompt fragment (always included)

Place the provided brand logo as a small, tasteful watermark in a lower corner. Do not distort it. Keep it subtle and consistent in the same corner every time.

## Refs (required)

- generations/refs/app-logo.png  (your real logo, passed as a reference, never described in words)

## Params

- inherit from the format preset you combine this with (for example pov)

## Usage

Combine with a format preset:
"/generate using the pov and app-watermark presets: you wake up as a caveman."
Every clip then carries your logo. When one pops, that is millions of impressions for your product.

## Note

For a hard, pixel-accurate watermark, add it in post instead, so it is identical on every clip. Use this preset when you want the logo baked into the scene naturally.
