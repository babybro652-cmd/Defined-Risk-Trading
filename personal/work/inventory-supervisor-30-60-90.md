# 30-60-90 Plan: Night Inventory Supervisor

For the Round 2 interview with Rose Simpson, Wed 11/11 2:30 PM.

## 20-second version (say this out loud)

"In my first 30 days I learn the people, the process and the numbers. By 60 days I standardize: a nightly handoff to day shift and a count plan aimed at the problem locations. By 90 days I want accuracy above the baseline we set together, and a backup lead trained on my team."

## Days 1-30: Learn

- Have a one-on-one with every person on the night team about their strengths, their pain points and what slows them down.
- Shadow each job: cycle counts, variance research, adjustments, damages and receiving checks.
- Learn the system reports: adjustments, variances, location accuracy and open tasks.
- Get baselines for:
  - inventory accuracy
  - location accuracy
  - adjustment dollars
  - damages and shrink
  - counts completed per night
  - overtime
- Meet the day inventory lead, receiving, replenishment and the freezer leads, and agree on what the handoff looks like.
- Do a safety walk and confirm the forklift certification is current.
- **Deliverable:** a one-page summary for my manager with the top 3 problems, plus an accuracy target we agree on.

## Days 31-60: Standardize

- Start a nightly handoff report to day shift:
  - counts done
  - open variances
  - damages
  - problem locations
- Build a daily count plan. Count high-volume, high-value and repeat-problem locations first.
- Root-cause the worst SKUs: recount, check neighboring locations, verify the SKU, check backstock and damages.
- Use my training background to write short job aids for counts and adjustments, and cross-train 2 people.
- Post team goals on a board and hold a 10-minute weekly huddle.
- **Deliverable:** a weekly trend of accuracy and adjustments against the baseline.

## Days 61-90: Own it

- Hit the accuracy target and cut repeat variances.
- Present before-and-after results to Rose, with a plan for the next 6 months.
- Develop a backup lead from the team.
- Propose one bigger fix, such as slotting, damage reduction or freezer date rotation (first in, first out).
- Recognize the team for the wins.

## If they ask about the day freezer production role

Same structure, different numbers:
- cases per hour
- labor per case
- food safety and temperature checks
- date codes and first in, first out
- cold-weather PPE and safety

## Tie it to your answers

- Your earlier question "What's inventory accuracy now, and where do you want it in 6 months?" is your day-30 target conversation.
- Your recount, neighbor locations, SKU, backstock and damages answer is your day-60 root-cause step.
- Training and Development is why the job aids and cross-training are believable.
