## 1979 Oldsmobile Cutlass Supreme (G-body, frame-off)

**Rendering:** dark pewter, full-glass roof, shaved and smoothed, flush glass, tucked bumpers,
full-width LED tail panel, twin square tips, LS forged twin turbo (turbos in the front corners of
the bay), intercooler behind the grille, 3 inch stainless duals with front dumps, slammed on
polished billet 19 front / 20 rear, mini-tubs, burgundy diamond-stitched leather into the trunk,
digital dash, 6-speed manual (the build keeps the 4L80E with a console shifter, 10/21; a future
re-render will show the console shifter), hidden trunk battery.

**Decided 10/21 (Tim):** 4L80E automatic with a console-mounted B&M Pro Stick shifter (T-56:
not chosen), coilovers (air ride: not chosen), RestoMod Air A/C (replaces Vintage Air), 7x6 LED
headlights. Details and how to confirm the headlight size: "Build decisions (Tim, 10/21)" in the
Cutlass notes.

### Closest match to the rendering

- **Stance:** coilovers (Tim, 10/21): Ridetech 11320301 G-body coil-over system with StrongArms.
  Air ride (Ridetech 11320298) sits lower parked but is not chosen (10/21).
- **Wheels:** Billet Specialties or Schott polished billet, 19x8 front, 20x10 rear (20x11/12 with
  tubs). Closest look to the smooth polished faces in the rendering.
- **Body:** no kit exists for this look. The rendering is custom metal work (shaved, flush glass,
  tucked bumpers, panoramic roof); every body line below is shop fabrication plus supplies.
- **Dash:** Holley 12.3 Pro Dash matches the full-screen digital dash in the rendering best and
  shares data with the Holley ECU. Dakota Digital makes the VHX analog-look kit for the 1978-88
  Cutlass (VHX-78O-CUT, checked, fits the stock bezel); no Cutlass RTX kit was found.

## 2016 Dodge Charger Pursuit (5.7 Hemi, RWD)

**Updated 10/23: build once.** The final engine is the plan, not an option: a forged 6.4 / 392
with a Whipple 3.0L twin-screw at about 7 psi (about 650-700 crank hp, about 620-660 lb-ft; traditional
supercharger, no ProCharger, Tim 10/23), the 8HP90, fuel,
cooling, axles and driveshaft all sized for that from day one and installed together with one
tune. Chassis and looks go first whenever money allows. The 5.7 gets oil changes only until
the swap. Look, widebody and remote start unchanged. Order and reasons: build-guides.md.

**Rendering:** white pearl widebody, flared fenders and quarters, front splitter, side skirts,
rear diffuser, ducktail, stock full-width racetrack tail, quad tips, black badges and grille,
LED headlights, slammed, dark bronze concave forged wheels, wide tires, big brakes.

**Ex-police items to plan for:** fill the A-pillar spotlight holes and any roof antenna and push
bar holes before paint; check for a factory engine oil cooler and heavy-duty cooling before
buying those parts (most Pursuits have them); 2015+ Mopar PCMs must be unlocked before an HP
Tuners or Diablo tune.

### Closest match to the rendering

- **Widebody:** Clinched Flares Charger widebody kit ($4,000 checked; ABS, +1.2 in front and
  +1.6 in rear per side, bolt-on, fits 2015-2023 Charger). Closest to the rendering's smooth flared
  fenders and quarters without cutting the quarters. Budget option: Vicrez Demon-style flares
  ($783 checked, 2015-2023 Charger; fit quality varies). Factory Hellcat widebody parts look the
  most OEM but the quarters must be cut; a Charger retrofit is Fit: confirm.
  **What's in each kit (checked 10/22):** Clinched = full kit, not just flares: front and rear
  fender flares, front bumper lip and bumper flares, side skirt add-ons, rear door add-ons;
  unpainted black ABS, taped and screwed on, about 2 hrs. American Muscle lists it for 2015-2023
  Charger SRT/Hellcat, so the bumper pieces may not fit the Pursuit's non-SRT front bumper: ask
  Clinched before buying. Vicrez Demon style (American Muscle, 10/22): flares only with a chin
  splitter $1,005 (vz102120); flares + front bumper + chin splitter $1,850 (vz102119); also sold
  with side skirts (vz102191, vz102192). The $783 price was an older listing.
- **Aero:** Function Factory ZL1 Addons R/T splitter, side skirts and rear extensions (2015-2021
  non-widebody styling; Fit: confirm with the widebody flares) plus a ducktail spoiler.
- **Wheels:** Vossen HF-5 or a forged Vossen S17 / Forgeline in satin or dark bronze, 20x10.5
  front, 20x11 rear, concave. Matches the bronze concave look.
