# Parts lists: all four builds (10/21/2026)

Tim asked (10/21): every upgrade and part each build needs, matched to the approved renderings,
with real brands and models. One section per vehicle. Prices are 2026 retail, rounded; ones
checked online on 10/21 say "checked". Everything else is a typical range: confirm before buying.
**"Fit: confirm"** means no listing confirmed that exact part for that exact model and year.

Columns: **Req** = required for the build as rendered; **Opt** = optional or one of two choices.
**DIY** = Tim can do it with hand tools; **Shop** = needs a shop, welder, paint booth or machine work.

Budget effects are at the end of each section and in `build-plan.md` (changed lines show "was").

## 1979 Oldsmobile Cutlass Supreme (G-body, frame-off)

**Rendering:** dark pewter, full-glass roof, shaved and smoothed, flush glass, tucked bumpers,
full-width LED tail panel, twin square tips, LS forged twin turbo (turbos in the front corners of
the bay), intercooler behind the grille, 3 inch stainless duals with front dumps, slammed on
polished billet 19 front / 20 rear, mini-tubs, burgundy diamond-stitched leather into the trunk,
digital dash, 6-speed manual (the build keeps the 4L80E with a console shifter, 10/21; a future
re-render will show the console shifter), hidden trunk battery.

**Decided 10/21 (Tim):** 4L80E automatic with a console-mounted B&M Pro Stick shifter (T-56:
not chosen), coilovers (air ride: not chosen), RestoMod Air A/C (replaces Vintage Air), 7x6 LED
headlights. Details and how to confirm the headlight size: "Build decisions (Tim, 10/21)" in the
Cutlass notes.

### Closest match to the rendering

- **Stance:** coilovers (Tim, 10/21): Ridetech 11320301 G-body coil-over system with StrongArms.
  Air ride (Ridetech 11320298) sits lower parked but is not chosen (10/21).
- **Wheels:** Billet Specialties or Schott polished billet, 19x8 front, 20x10 rear (20x11/12 with
  tubs). Closest look to the smooth polished faces in the rendering.
- **Body:** no kit exists for this look. The rendering is custom metal work (shaved, flush glass,
  tucked bumpers, panoramic roof); every body line below is shop fabrication plus supplies.
- **Dash:** Holley 12.3 Pro Dash matches the full-screen digital dash in the rendering best and
  shares data with the Holley ECU. Dakota Digital makes the VHX analog-look kit for the 1978-88
  Cutlass (VHX-78O-CUT, checked, fits the stock bezel); no Cutlass RTX kit was found.

## 2012 Chevy Cruze LT RS (1.4T, 182,995 miles)

**Rendering:** gloss black, bagged and aired out, 18 inch gloss black wheels with stretch and
camber, LED halo projector headlights, smoked LED taillights, front lip, side skirts, rear
diffuser, single large tip, black bowties and grille, upgraded turbo.

**Tim, 10/21:** the car is white now; he wants the render's gloss black, so the color change is
in the build and the budget (wrap chosen, paint priced as the option). Interior accents change
from red to burnt orange: stitching, trim accents, ambient lighting. Smoked taillights: yes.

**What 182,995 miles changes:** the head is off now, so finish the head job first and have the
machine shop check the head and deck. Before a bigger turbo, run compression and leak-down after
the rebuild and check for oil burning; the 1.4T's cast pistons and ringlands are the weak point
under more boost. A bigger turbo on a tired bottom end risks the engine. Sensible order: head
job, then air ride, wheels and lights (the look), then the turbo only if the engine tests healthy.

### Closest match to the rendering

- **Air ride:** Air Lift Performance does not list a Cruze kit (no listing found). The render
  match is a complete Cruze kit built on Air Lift Performance 3P or 3H management with Universal
  Air (UAS) bags and Ksport struts (Cruze Culture complete 3P kit for 2011-16, about $2,850 on an
  old listing), or AirREX front and rear struts for the 2011 Cruze ($3,100 checked) with 3P/3H.
  Bag Riders sells 1st-gen Cruze kits too. Both lay the car on the ground like the rendering.
- **Headlights:** Spyder Auto black projector headlights with LED halo for 2011-2014 Cruze
  ($328–350 checked). Fits the 2012.
- **Wheels:** the Cruze is 5x105, a rare bolt pattern. Either buy 5x105 wheels or run
  5x105 to 5x112/5x114.3 adapters (15-20 mm) to open up wheel choices.
