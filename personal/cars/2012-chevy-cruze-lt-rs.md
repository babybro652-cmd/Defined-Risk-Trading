## Repair in progress: head off (10/12/2026)

**Why the head came off (Tim, 10/21/2026): burnt valve.**
- Head is at the machine shop (Tim, 10/21). Ask them to: replace the burnt valve, cut or replace its seat, check every valve and seat (vacuum test), guides, and head flatness; pressure test for cracks. Ask for a full valve job while it's there. Budget about $300-700.
- Check the piston and cylinder under the burnt valve for damage before reassembly.
- Fix the cause or it burns again. Common 1.4T causes: running lean from a vacuum leak (the valve cover/PCV is a known failure), a bad injector or coil on that cylinder, overheating, or long misfire. Replace the valve cover with PCV, check that cylinder's injector and coil, and smoke-test for vacuum leaks after reassembly.
- After it runs: compression and leak-down on all 4 cylinders as the baseline for the rebuilt engine (10/23: no longer the turbo gate; the engine is built for it).

Cylinder head removed. Block deck and pistons need cleaning before reassembly.

Deck cleaning plan (iron block, aluminum head; same care on both):
- No Roloc, Scotch-Brite or wire wheel on a drill; they cut aluminum and leave grit that reaches the bearings.
- Plug oil and coolant holes with rags, tape bolt holes, grease the top of each cylinder wall to catch debris.
- Gasket remover spray, wait 10-15 min, then a plastic scraper or a razor held nearly flat.
- Pistons: bring each near the top, soak carbon, scrape with plastic, wipe.
- Vacuum, brake-clean until rags come off clean, re-oil the cylinder walls.
- Blow out every head bolt hole (fluid left in a hole can crack the block on torque-down).
- Check deck flatness with a straightedge and feeler gauge; over ~0.002 in (0.05 mm) needs machining.

Reassembly notes:
- New torque-to-yield head bolts (single use).
- New head gasket, installed dry.
- Have a machine shop check the head for warp.
