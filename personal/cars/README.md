# Tim's cars

**End goal for every car (Tim, 10/12/2026): high-performance street car with aggressive styling.** Builds go in phases: reliable first, then power, then looks.

**Standard on every car (Tim, 10/12/2026):** oil catch can(s), oil cooler, baffled oil pan, braided stainless brake lines, multi-piston big brake kit, coilovers (Cruze: air suspension instead), upgraded sway bars, and AN fittings/lines on all major systems (fuel, oil, coolant, turbo/supercharger feeds and returns).

One file per car: specs, maintenance log, repairs in progress, parts bought.
