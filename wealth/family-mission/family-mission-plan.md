## Crest concepts (to turn into images once Tim answers)

A crest has 5 parts: shield, central symbol, colors, supporters or border, and a motto banner.

**Concept A: The Guardian**
- Shield: split in two, deep navy and gold.
- Center: a pit bull head in profile (loyalty, protection, strength), facing forward.
- Above: a small crown of three points (three generations).
- Motto banner: "Build. Protect. Pass it on."

**Concept B: The Builder**
- Shield: navy with a gold chevron (the classic symbol for builders and protectors of a house).
- Center: an oak tree with deep roots (long-term growth, family tree), with the year the family book starts (2026).
- Bottom corners: two small keys (owning your home, owning your time).
- Motto banner: "Freedom is built, not given."

**Concept C: The Engine**
- Shield: black and burnt orange (matches Tim's car and brand colors).
- Center: a stylized "W" made of two crossed wrenches or pistons (work ethic, building with your hands).
- Top: a rising sun (new start, his son's generation).
- Motto banner: "Patience. Discipline. Legacy."

Color meanings (classic heraldry): gold = generosity and wisdom; navy/blue = loyalty and truth; black = constancy; red or orange = strength and courage; green = growth and hope.
